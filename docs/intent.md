# Intent — Why I'm Building This

## The problem

People find me on LinkedIn, GitHub, X and other social apps. But a social bio only has room for one line. Nobody can understand what I've actually built from one line.

Right now, if someone wants to know what I'm worth, they have to dig through my resume, my GitHub and my LinkedIn separately. Most people won't do that.

## What I want

One link I can put in every social media bio.

When anyone opens it, within **5 seconds** they should know:

1. **Who I am** — Rohit Srivastava, a frontend engineer.
2. **What I'm good at** — building fast interfaces for real-time, data-heavy products.
3. **Proof** — real numbers, not claims (1M+ traders use my QR Login, 4s → under 2s chart load, 60fps live maps, 2 industry firsts).
4. **Where I am right now** — what I'm building today, not just what I did years ago.

## The feeling I want people to have

> "This guy is good, he ships real things, and he's still levelling up."

Not "another developer portfolio." It should feel **premium, calm and confident**, like an Apple product page.

## Who will open it

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** — want to see impact quickly.
- **Other developers** — want to see projects and code.
- **Friends and followers** — just curious what I do.
- **Possible collaborators or clients** — want a way to contact me.

All of them should get what they need without hunting for it.

## Why the "journey" matters

A list of jobs is boring. My story is a climb, from college at NIT Jalandhar to building industry-first features at FYERS to real-time systems at Intellicar.

So I'm showing it as a **road with levels**, like a game map. Each stop unlocks new skills. It ends at "You are here" with the next level still loading. That tells people I'm still growing, which is the main message.

## Why placeholders are okay

Some projects aren't finished yet. Instead of hiding them, I show them as **"In the lab"**. That shows I'm always building something. When a project is ready, its card simply gets filled in.

## What success looks like

- I put this link in all my bios.
- Someone opens it on their phone and understands me in 5 seconds.
- They scroll through the journey because it's fun.
- They click LinkedIn, GitHub or email to reach me.
